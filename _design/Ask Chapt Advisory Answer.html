Today · plain prose

High-impact ways to make a chapter ops dashboard like ChaptOS better:

  • Home that's truly "next actions": upcoming mandatory events, next deadlines, overdue items, who's at-risk, and one-tap nudges to assignees.
  • Attendance UX: QR check-in, late cutoff, excused workflow, and "missed streaks" + auto-follow-ups.
  • Dues + treasury flow: payment requests, approval queue, auto-reconciliation, and clear separation of "owed vs pending vs paid."
  • Better search: "when is car wash?", "who missed last chapter?", "how much did we spend on socials this semester?" in one bar.
  • Notifications that aren't spammy: per-role digests, due-soon reminders, and escalation when items go overdue.
  • Programming pipeline: idea → planning → confirmed → done with owners, dependencies, and calendar sync.
  • Customization: custom metrics, thresholds, vocabulary, and member fields that drive "At Risk/Watch/Good."
  • Exports + audit trail: CSV/PDF exports for treasurer, and an activity log that explains changes.
