Hitting the limit

Only growth stops.

Go past your plan and you can't add the next person. Nothing else changes.

What stops

  • Approving requests past capacity (people can still send them).
  • Un-archiving someone.

What doesn't

  • Every member keeps access.
  • Every page keeps working.
  • Exports keep working.

Questions

Asked before paying.

Is this the same as the dues we track?

No. This is what your org pays us. The dues and ledger you keep in the app never touch it.

Who handles billing?

Your org admins. They add the card, change the plan and see the invoices.

We're bigger than 120.

Billing asks for a quote instead of a card, and nothing stops working meanwhile.

We run more than one org.

Admins of two or more orgs can ask about a group plan from Billing.
