How it works

Plan it once. Everyone’s in the loop.

01 · Timeline

Every meeting and event on one line.

Meetings, events, socials and deadlines live on a Timeline the whole org can read. Members can subscribe so it shows up in their own Apple or Google calendar.

Syncs to Apple & Google Calendar
02 · Attendance

Take attendance in one tap.

Open check-in when the meeting starts and members tap in from their phones. Attendance goes on each member’s record, so you finally know who’s actually active.

Excuses reviewed by officers
03 · Tasks

Hand it to a person, a role, or everyone.

Give a task to Jordan, to whoever is Treasurer, or to the whole roster at once. Tasks given to a role stay with the role, so next year’s treasurer picks them up automatically.

Members who join later get it too
04 · Money

A budget the whole board can follow.

Allocations, receipts and reimbursements go in one ledger. If your org collects dues, those payments post there too.
