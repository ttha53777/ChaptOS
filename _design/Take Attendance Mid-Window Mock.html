New orgs that never open a window see no change

Logging attendance after the meeting still starts with everyone ticked. For a full room, unticking the three who missed is the fastest way to work. That default was only dangerous when check-ins arrived at the same time, and saving only changes makes that safe. "All present" and "Clear" still work: they count as changing every row.
