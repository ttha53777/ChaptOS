Born with a term.

Today a founder finishes the interview, lands in their new workspace, and the first thing they see is a wall demanding a semester label and two dates. This mock folds that question into the interview itself — one more chip, asked in a place that already felt like a conversation — so the term exists before they ever arrive, and the wall never fires.

Two taps, zero typing

The model question narrows the vocabulary; the follow-up offers ready-made terms computed from today. A blank date field never appears.

The suggestion shows its work

Each chip carries its span in mono, and the sheet's bar marks where today falls inside it — so the guess is legible, not magic.

Wrong is cheap

Free text still works

The composer stays live. "we run Sept to June" is a perfectly good answer — the beat's chips are the fast path, not the only one.

Scope. Front-end concept only — the interview beat and the blueprint card. Backend wiring, schema, the dashboard checklist and the invite flow are all out of scope for this pass.
