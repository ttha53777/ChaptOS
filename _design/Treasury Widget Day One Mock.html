A welcome with one way forward

  • One button, one place. "Set up the treasury" goes to /treasury, where the opening-balance step (TreasuryOpenBooks) already runs. The dashboard and the page share one setup flow.
  • Beginner words. It says what the page is for (dues, expenses, balance) and what you need to start. No "ledger" or "transactions" yet.
  • Low stakes. "About two minutes · you can change it later" tells a first-time treasurer they can't get this wrong.
  • No numbers. The illustration is an open book waiting for its first line, so there's no $0 and no chart that hints at a direction.
  • Shown only with MANAGE_TREASURY. Everyone else sees state 03.

A real number, honestly unfinished

  • The balance gets full ink. The treasurer stated it, so it's a real figure, and a "Books open" tag confirms the step worked.
  • No projection. With no entries, the projection equals the balance, so repeating it adds nothing. It comes back once trend.length > 0.
  • A starting point, not a chart. One dot on the opening date and a flat dashed hairline. It never draws a curve, because no data means no direction.
  • Once there's a first entry, the card becomes today's populated card with no changes.
