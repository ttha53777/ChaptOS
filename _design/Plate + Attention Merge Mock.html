One list for everything waiting on you.

"Needs attention" (the officer queue: overdue deadlines, wrap-ups, reimbursements, dues) and "On your plate" (your own tasks and votes) were two cards asking the same question. Here they're one card, sorted by when it's due, where every row has one button for the next step.

1
Sorted by when, not by typeLate, then today, then coming up. You never have to work out which card to check.
2
One button per rowThe button says what happens: Mark done, Vote, Review, Wrap up. Those finish inside the card; dues opens the Dues panel.
3
It says why it's yoursOfficer items carry an "as Treasurer" tag. Members only see their own, so there are no tags.
4
Tasks can be undoneFinished rows go to "Just finished" at the bottom. A task can be reopened there; votes and approvals are final.
