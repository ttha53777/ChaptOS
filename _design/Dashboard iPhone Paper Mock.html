Tonight goes first.The web brief's hero button becomes a ticket. Its one action — open check-in — sits in the thumb zone, then the ticket turns into the live count. The island carries it while you scroll.
Sheets, not pages.Every form slides up from the bottom and stacks: quick add → keypad, member → record payment. Drag the grabber to put it back. The one exception is an event’s More details: a full page that pushes in from the right.
Swipe to act.Needs attention rows swipe left for Snooze and their own action. A full swipe does it. Resolved rows still drop off, like the web.
The Timeline stays one line.The web spine and rail fold into one column: month heads stick under the nav, the rail’s detail becomes a sheet, and a “Today” pill brings you back. Line or Month, one tap.
Chapter is a minute book.More → Chapter pushes in from the right and keeps the tab bar. Tonight’s agenda is the hero, because that’s what you open it for at 7:29. Minutes get the whole screen, and writing them swaps the tab bar for a § · ☐ @ row.
Tasks deals you a hand.On a phone you open Tasks to see what’s on you, so your open ones are index cards, next on top: Done stamps it, Next shuffles. A sealed ballot sits under it, then the whole board by urgency. Tick a row, or swipe it left to push a day.
Measures are a deck.Four stat cards don't fit across 390px, so they swipe, with health leading. Each one opens its own breakdown.
