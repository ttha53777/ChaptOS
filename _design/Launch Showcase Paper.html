Seven things every student org runs on.

One club, start to finish. Hilltop Robotics charters on a Tuesday, has 42 members by October, and never opens a spreadsheet. Every plate plays when you reach it, and each one picks up where the last left off.

01 Day one · about a minute

Charter it in a minute. Let people ask to join.

Name the org, pick what you track, and share one link in the group chat. Nobody lands on the roster until an officer says yes.

  • The org type sets up roles and pages for you
  • One invite link and no typing names in by hand
  • Approve or decline, and the seat count stays honest

    02 Events · one calendar

    • Event kinds that fit your org, not a fixed list
    • Every member sees the same calendar
    • Required events track attendance automatically

    03 Attendance · live

    Roll call that takes itself.

    Open check-in and members tap I'm here on their phones. Excused absences are counted, you can see who's missing, and the result is recorded when you close it.

    • No paper sheet and no Google Form
    • Approved excuses don't count against anyone
    • Rates go straight to each member's record

    04 Treasury · dues

    Know who owes, no spreadsheet.

    Every dollar in and out goes into one ledger. Log a payment and the balance, the dues meter and the who-owes list all update together.

    • Categories that fit your org, like parts, sponsors and pizza
    • Dues collected vs. billed, always current
    • Open books so members can see where money went

    05 Meetings · minutes

    Minutes that turn into work.

    Start from your org's agenda template and take notes as the meeting goes. Every action item you write becomes a task with an owner and a due date.

    • Shared templates for weekly meetings, exec board and elections
    • Attendance comes from the check-in
    • @mention someone and the task is theirs

    06 Tasks · roles

    Everyone does their part. Roles hand out the keys.

    Assign a task to everyone and each member checks off their own. Give someone a role and the pages that come with it unlock, with no per-person permission settings.

    • "Everyone, each" tasks track every member's part
    • Roles carry permissions, and people carry roles
    • Hand over a role at elections and access follows it

    07 Ask Chapt · ⌘K

    Ask your org anything.

    Ask in plain English and get answers from your own records, with the working shown. When it suggests a change, someone with the right role approves it before anything happens.

    • Reads dues, attendance, events and tasks together
    • Shows which records it checked
    • Drafts the follow-up, and a person approves it
