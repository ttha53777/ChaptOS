  1. A tab bar on every org page. Home, Timeline, +, the roster and More, which opens the existing sidebar drawer. The badge on the roster tab is the join queue.
  2. + is Create. The same actions Quick actions shows you on desktop, plus Open check-in and the pinned note. On another page it brings you home with that form open.
  3. The briefing keeps the greeting and loses its chips. My standing is your face in the header, and Arrange stays on desktop.
  4. Three numbers on one row, then Up next, your plate and the week, in that order whatever the desktop column order is.
  5. Treasury, Brothers, Activity and Docs fold into tiles showing one number or line each. A tap opens the real card in a sheet you can drag down to close.
  6. Ask Chapt shrinks to its spark and sits above the tab bar.

Safari: the page fills 100dvh so nothing hides under the toolbar, the bar pads for the home indicator with env(safe-area-inset-bottom), and hover styles are only for real pointers. On a phone, open this file directly to see it full-screen without the frame.
