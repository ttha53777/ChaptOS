  3. + in the tab bar is New task, opened as a bottom sheet. The people row scrolls sideways instead of wrapping.
  4. The Tasks tab carries your overdue count, so you see it from any page.
  5. Dates move under the title (“2 days late”), and the calendar block stays on the right.
