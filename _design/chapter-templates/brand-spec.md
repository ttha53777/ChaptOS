## Mock scope
A standalone, local prototype. Templates are scoped to the single fictional organization and saved only in this browser. No API or production changes.
The user's “creating form” is interpreted as the Add meeting form on Chapter. Picking a template copies its text into editable meeting notes. Editing a copy never modifies the template.
