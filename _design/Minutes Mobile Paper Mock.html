What changed

  1. The header never leaves. Back, the live dot, who's here and ⋯ sit in a bar outside the scroller, and the page is sized to what's visible (visualViewport), so neither Safari's toolbar nor the keyboard can push it off.
  2. The margin becomes tabs. Desktop's pad + margin (Summary, Attendance) is Minutes · Summary · Who came. The meta strip becomes a ticket with the meeting clock.
  3. Typing folds the chrome. When the keyboard opens the ticket and tabs fold away, the pad takes the room, and the caret is kept above the keyboard.
  4. Formatting is one tap, not syntax. An accessory bar on the keyboard: § heading, • point, ☐ to-do, @ mention, B, I and Done. Typing ## , - or [] still works. Return continues a list; Return on an empty point ends it.
  5. @ is a chip strip, not a popup. People and roles scroll sideways above the keyboard where your thumb already is.
  6. You can see who else is writing. Their caret carries a flag in the pad, the header says "Nia is writing in Philanthropy", and Who's here can jump to them.
  7. Big targets at the bottom. Write and Summarize live in a thumb dock while the keyboard is down.

Open this file on a phone (or any window ≤600px) and it drops the frame and uses your real keyboard. ?frame forces the frame. New vs desktop: the ticket's meeting clock bar and "Jump to" in Who's here; everything else maps to something the overlay already does.
