Ask Chapt · reply states · exploration

Eleven new ways to answer.

Today every structured answer is a verdict plus a ruled list of rows. That's right for who and which — and wrong when the honest picture is one number, a share, a direction, a week, a quote, or “I couldn't find it.” Each state here is an optional shape on the answer: the model picks the shape and writes the verdict; the server fills every number from tools Ask Chapt already calls. Older app builds ignore shape and keep rendering the rows.
